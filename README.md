# ProteinBlast – Protein & Nutrition Tracker

ProteinBlast is an easy-to-use protein and nutrition tracking application designed to help users monitor their daily protein intake and achieve their fitness goals.

Users can log their daily food consumption, track protein from different food sources, and monitor their total protein intake against their personalized daily protein target. The application provides a clear breakdown of protein consumed through individual meals, helping users understand their nutritional habits and make better dietary decisions.

**Key Features:**
- **Daily Protein Tracking:** Monitor total protein intake throughout the day.
- **Food Logging:** Add food items and record their protein content.
- **Personalized Protein Goals:** Set daily protein targets based on individual fitness goals.
- **Meal-wise Tracking:** Track protein intake for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.
- **Progress Dashboard:** Visualize daily progress toward protein goals.
- **Nutrition Insights:** Understand your eating habits and identify protein gaps.
- **Fitness Support:** Helpful for muscle building, weight management, and maintaining a balanced diet.

**Goal:** Make protein tracking simple, accurate, and accessible so users can stay consistent with their nutrition and fitness journey.
